Zakir Khan

Zakir Khan is an Indian stand-up comedian, writer, actor, and poet known for his Hindi storytelling style that blends humor and emotion. Rising from modest beginnings in Indore, he has become one of India’s most popular comedy voices, performing globally and headlining sold-out shows, including at Madison Square Garden in 2025.

Key Facts

  • Born: August 20, 1987, Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India
  • Notable work: Haq Se Single, Tathastu, Chacha Vidhayak Hain Humare
  • Major milestone: First Indian to headline a Hindi comedy show at Madison Square Garden in 2025
  • Awards: Comedy Central India’s Best Stand-Up Comedian, 2012

Early Life and Background

Born into a family with a rich musical legacy, Khan is the grandson of classical musician Ustad Moinuddin Khan and son of music teacher Ismail Khan. Initially trained in the sitar, Zakir’s artistic roots later found expression in humor and storytelling. His education included radio programming in Delhi, which led to work as a radio jockey and writer before his breakthrough in comedy.

Rise to Fame

Khan’s popularity surged after winning Comedy Central’s India’s Best Stand-Up Comedian contest in 2012. His relatable observations on middle-class life and relationships, delivered in colloquial Hindi, resonated with young audiences. Viral clips such as Life Me Chahiye Izzat and his Haq Se Single special cemented his status as a cultural voice for a new generation of Indian humorists.

Major Works and Style

Zakir Khan’s signature mix of wit and vulnerability shines in specials like Tathastu and the show Chacha Vidhayak Hain Humare, where he portrays an underdog navigating small-town politics. He is also the creator of the spoken-word series Farzi Mushaira, showcasing his affinity for Urdu and Hindi poetry. His comedic approach often reflects introspection, self-deprecation, and social commentary.

Global Recognition

In August 2025, Khan made history as the first Indian to perform an entirely Hindi stand-up show at New York’s Madison Square Garden, receiving a standing ovation and calling his parents on stage during the emotional finale. This milestone underscored his role in bringing Hindi-language comedy to global platforms.

Anubhav Singh Bassi

Anubhav Singh Bassi is one of India’s most popular Hindi stand-up comedians, known for storytelling comedy based on hostel life, friendships, UPSC preparation, law school, and everyday struggles.

Key Facts

  • Born: January 9, 1991
  • From: Meerut, Uttar Pradesh, India
  • Background: Lawyer, UPSC aspirant, entrepreneur, comedian
  • Comedy style: Anecdotal, observational, storytelling comedy
  • Started stand-up: Around 2017 after performing at an open mic

Famous Comedy Sets

  • Hostel
  • Cheating
  • Waxing
  • UPSC
  • Roommate

His videos have received hundreds of millions of views on YouTube.

Why People Like Him

  • College and hostel nostalgia
  • Chaotic friend groups
  • Career confusion
  • Middle-class Indian experiences

Recent Appearances and News

  • Appeared on a comedy special episode of Kaun Banega Crorepati with other comedians.
  • Continues doing live tours across India and international shows.

Kenneth Mathew Sebastian

Kenny Sebastian is an Indian stand-up comedian, musician, filmmaker, and podcaster known for clean observational comedy, musical humor, and relatable storytelling.

Key Facts

  • Born: December 31, 1990
  • From: Kerala; raised in Bengaluru
  • Language: Primarily English, with Hindi and Malayalam elements
  • Comedy style: Observational, musical, anecdotal, improvisational

Why He Became Famous

Kenny first gained popularity on YouTube through:

  • Comedy sketches
  • Musical parody videos
  • The viral #KennySing4Me series

He later became one of India’s biggest English-language stand-up comics through live tours, Amazon Prime specials, Netflix specials, and judging roles on Comicstaan.

What Audiences Like About Him

  • Clean and family-friendly comedy
  • Musical bits with guitar
  • Awkward social humor
  • Calm storytelling style

Harsh Gujral

Harsh Gujral is an Indian stand-up comedian and YouTuber known for energetic crowd work, relatable Hindi comedy, and loud interactive performances.

Key Facts

  • Born: September 17, 1993
  • From: Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India
  • Background: Former engineer turned comedian
  • Comedy style: Crowd work, observational humor, roast-style interactions, Hindi stand-up

Why He Became Popular

Harsh gained massive popularity through:

  • Viral YouTube comedy clips
  • Live audience roasting
  • Relatable middle-class Indian humor

Some of his most famous sets include:

  • Shadi Ki Rasmein
  • Sasta Vicky Kaushal
  • Kanpur and River Ganga
  • My Name Is Harsh Gujral

Comedy Style

He is especially known for:

  • Aggressive crowd interaction
  • Spontaneous jokes with audiences
  • Wedding and relationship humor
  • High-energy delivery

Fans often compare his style to roast comedy and live improv.

Public Reactions

Audience opinions about Harsh Gujral are divided online. Some fans praise his desi crowd-work humor, while others criticize his loud or crass style. Common reactions include “funny, relatable” and “too cringe.”
